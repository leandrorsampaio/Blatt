ATS

Job portals pass your CV through an applicant tracking system (ATS) that pulls the text out of the PDF. Here you set what the PDF says about itself, check what the machine finds, and see the order in which it reads your CV.

PDF properties

Saved with this CV version. Leave a field empty and it follows your CV automatically.

Upload a Text PDF to job portals (Download PDF → Print / Save as Text PDF); the one-column version is the safest, and portals that ask for Word get the Word document. Browsers only let a Text PDF carry the title and the language, and most of them suggest the title as its file name. The Image PDF carries every property and a hidden copy of the text, but that copy only holds Western European letters. Keywords are rarely used by an ATS: the words in your CV are what count.
