Blatt
/blat/ · German, noun

“Blatt” is the German word for a sheet of paper — a single leaf, a single page. It's the whole idea behind this project: your CV is one sheet, and the app that makes it is one file. Nothing to install, nothing running in the background, nothing sent anywhere. Just you and your page.

From download to done in minutes

No account to create, no template store to browse. Just three steps.

1

Download & open

Grab the ZIP, extract it, and double-click cv.html. It opens like any web page.

2

Edit & style

Fill in your details in the friendly editor, then tune colors, fonts and spacing live.

3

Export

Download a clean A4 PDF with one click — or print it ATS-friendly. Your work auto-saves.
